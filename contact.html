What to prepare

Photographs in natural light (overall, detail of damage, any maker marks). Approximate dimensions. Timeline and whether the piece must remain in service during planning.

For automotive work: year, model, VIN if available, and whether originality or upgraded comfort is the priority.

For trade: firm name, project address, and GC schedule if applicable.
