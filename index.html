02 — Methodology
The Five Phases.
01
01
Strip & Assessment

Complete deconstruction to the frame. Structural assessment, damage mapping, and material analysis. Nothing hidden.

02
02
Frame Restoration

Joint reinforcement, kiln-dried hardwood replacement. The skeleton must perform for decades before a single layer is added.

03
03
Material Selection

Curated consultation. We match material to use-case, aesthetic vision, and longevity requirements. No shortcuts.

04
04
Precision Upholstery

Hand-cut, hand-stitched. Double-lock stitch at 12 threads per inch. Every seam tension-tested. Every corner hand-pulled.

05
05
Final Detail

Full inspection under studio lighting. Finishing treatment applied. Photographic documentation before delivery.
