The Process

02 - Methodology

The Five
Phases.

Engineering-grade precision applied to artisan restoration. Every phase is documented, every detail is accountable.

01 *

Strip & Assessment

Complete deconstruction to the frame. Structural assessment, damage mapping, and material analysis. Nothing hidden.

02 *

Frame Restoration

Joint reinforcement, kiln-dried hardwood replacement where needed. The skeleton must perform for decades before we add a single layer.

03 *

Material Selection

Curated consultation. We match material to use-case, aesthetic vision, and longevity requirements. No shortcuts in this phase.

04 *

Precision Upholstery

Hand-cut, hand-stitched. Double-lock stitch at 12 threads per inch. Every seam tension-tested. Every corner hand-pulled and set.

05 *

Final Detail

Full inspection under studio lighting. Finishing treatment applied. Photographic documentation before delivery. Perfection, verified.

04 - The Workshop

We work with interior designers, architects, collectors, and families across the Bay Area who understand that quality is a decision made at the start of a project, not the end.
