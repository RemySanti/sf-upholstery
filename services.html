Engagement model

How a commission begins

1. Intake conversation. We review imagery, provenance, usage, and timeline. No blind estimates - context drives engineering.

2. Studio examination. Pieces are evaluated on the bench under raking light. You receive a written scope: frame, suspension, comfort layers, and finish.

3. Material sign-off. Swatches are approved in your space when needed; large hides are inspected for grain continuity before cutting.

4. Bench production. Single-team accountability - the same master craftsperson often shepherds a piece from pattern through final detail.

5. Delivery & care. Conditioning instructions, photographic record, and optional revisit for seasonal leather care in the first year.
