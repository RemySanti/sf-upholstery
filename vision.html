Creative Direction  —  Production Brief

Client
Vision.

A cinematic production framework — defining the visual language, commercial environments, and luxury aesthetic for SF Upholstery Group's brand story.

01 — Core Objective

Showcase Capability
& Luxury Positioning.

Create cinematic visuals that demonstrate what SF Upholstery Group is capable of across high-end commercial environments — where craft, scale, and atmosphere converge.

Focus — 01
Luxury Craftsmanship

Every frame communicates obsessive attention to material and finish. Stitching, texture, and surface detail rendered cinematically.

Focus — 02
Commercial-Scale Work

Demonstrating capacity for large hospitality and institutional installations — not bespoke one-offs, but full environments.

Focus — 03
Premium Hospitality Aesthetics

Luxury dining, VIP lounge, and five-star lobby environments as the visual context for SFUG's work.

Focus — 04
Custom Fabrics & Finishes

Bespoke material stories — velvet, leather, curtain-grade fabric — shot in macro with cinematic lighting to communicate exclusivity.

Focus — 05
Fire-Retardant & Theater-Grade

Technical authority positioned as a luxury differentiator. Commercial-grade safety meets premium visual design.

Focus — 06
Cinematic Atmosphere

Upscale lifestyle visuals that position SFUG inside a world of considered luxury — not just a vendor, but a creative partner.

Technical
05 — Technical & Material Demonstrations

Fire-Retardant.
Visually Stunning.

Position commercial-grade safety standards as a luxury differentiator — professionalism made visually cinematic through elegant technical overlays and material sequences.

FR
Fire Retardant Visuals

Fabric treatment sequences that communicate professional-grade safety standards through elegance — not industrial footage. The visual language of trust and compliance.

◎
Slow-Motion Fabric Movement

High-speed capture of fabric in motion — the fall of curtain-weight velvet, the tension of a leather pull, the cascade of performance linen. Material poetry.

≡
Technical Overlays

Elegant graphic overlays — thread counts, FR ratings, Wyzenbeek scores — presented with cinematic restraint. Authority without compromise to the aesthetic.

✦
Craftsmanship Sequences

Detailed macro shots of hand-stitching, material testing, and finish application. The process elevated to the level of the finished piece.
