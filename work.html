Portfolio

Work that reads as collectible.

Featured projects from 2020-2026. Full case files, including strip-down photography and material memos, are available to prospective clients under NDA during consultation.

Residential - Antique The Edwardian Club Chair

1908 frame; mortise reglue and corner blocking. Eight-way hand tie rebuilt. Full-grain aniline in cognac with hand-tufted deck. ~180 bench hours.

Commercial - Hospitality Pacific Heights Hotel Lounge

Fourteen-piece program. Performance velvet, contract foam package, reinforced corner blocks. Scheduled install across two overnight windows.

Bespoke - Contemporary Noe Valley Sectional

Custom frame and suspension for a 11 ft run. Oatmeal linen, contrast oxide welt, integrated USB-free tailoring at client request.

Automotive - Interior 1972 Mercedes 280SE

Perforated Nappa seating surfaces, Alcantara headliner, period stitch mapping. Cabin acoustics preserved with original pad strategy.

Residential - Collector Pierre Jeanneret Homage Pair

Cane replacement with traditional six-way weave; teak oil finish rebuild. Upholstered in vegetable-tanned leather with hand-burnished edges.

Design Trade - Repeat Client Presidio Heights Dining Program

Twelve chairs and two host armchairs. Leather skirts with invisible stretch panels; brass nail schedule aligned to leg sight lines.
